Privacy Policy

Pip's Pocket Adventures is designed for children and families. This policy explains what information the app uses, what it does not ask for, and the limited services used to provide subscriptions.

Information we do not ask children to provide

The app does not require a child account and does not ask for a child's name, birthday, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on the device

Learning progress, earned badges and narration preferences are stored locally on the device. Families can reset this information from the app's Grown-up Corner or remove it by uninstalling the app.

Camera, microphone and location

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts or precise location. Text-to-speech narration is produced by the device's installed speech service and is controlled by the family's device settings.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, behavioural advertising or social features. It does not use a child profile for advertising and does not send learning progress, scores or drawings to advertising services.

Subscriptions and purchase verification

A grown-up can choose an optional monthly or yearly subscription to unlock premium learning worlds. Apple or Google processes the payment, and the app does not receive payment-card details.

RevenueCat is used to confirm and restore subscriptions. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user identifier and purchase, app, platform and device information needed to provide that service. The app does not provide RevenueCat with a child's name, contact details, learning progress, scores or drawings. Advertising identifiers and RevenueCat diagnostics are disabled in the app.

Internet access

Story artwork and learning content are bundled with the app. When internet access is available, the app may request a small public seasonal-theme configuration file hosted on GitHub. That request does not include a child's name, score, drawing, learning progress or other in-app activity. A bundled theme is used while offline.

Children's privacy

We design the app to minimise data collection from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Data retention and deletion

Local app progress remains on the device until it is reset or the app is removed. Purchase records are retained by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as needed to process, verify and restore subscriptions, subject to their own policies and legal obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

Contact us
